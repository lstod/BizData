7 of 18 over ceiling; across the full seed set the median active engagement had already

VERIFICATION

All 17 seeds, after the change: 8 of 8 mess cases detected, 7 of 7 health assertions pass.
Two defects surfaced during the change and were fixed rather than tuned around:

  1. Case 6 (ends mid-period) went silent for its last fortnight, because roll-off jitter
     took the supporting team off early and the engagement lead carries the lowest weight.
     The case-3 detector then found two silent engagements instead of one. Fixed by
     holding the team on to the end date for that engagement.

  2. Case 4 scored red with burn_trajectory as its top risk — the opposite of the healthy
     burn it exists to show. Burn to date and projected burn divide the same ceiling, so
     the ratio between them is a property of the engagement, not something the ceiling can
     set. Fixed by anchoring case 4 on the fixed-fee engagement with the least runway left
     relative to work already delivered, so both land in the healthy band.
