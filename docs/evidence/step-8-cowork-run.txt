  The third one is the one worth reading closely. Engagements 8 and 9 are both
  past their ceilings, and the recommendation on each is to "raise a change
  order" -- not to raise the ceiling. That is the distinction the rule exists
  for, and the model drew it unprompted.

  Engagement 25 is the other. Twenty-four days of silence, and the action reads:
  "Confirm with the delivery team whether work is ongoing and timesheets are
  outstanding, or whether delivery has in fact stopped; the absence of entries
  does not distinguish the two." Absent data treated as absent data.
