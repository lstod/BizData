CAVEAT — THESE SCREENSHOTS ARE NOT DEMO MATERIAL

The extreme burn figures are the known ceiling_hours generator defect from
step 2, not a designed mess case:

  active engagements          18
  already over ceiling_hours   7  (39%)
  worst burn               218.8%  (Fernhollow — Pricing Rollout)

scripts/generator.py draws ceiling_hours from randrange(200, 3201, 20) with no
relationship to planned effort, so a portfolio where 39% of engagements are over
budget is a seeding artifact. The client behaved correctly on the data it was
given. A delivery director would not find this portfolio credible, so the
ceiling_hours fix has to land before any demo pack is built on these numbers —
as build-1-plan.md already sequences it.

What this evidence proves: the plumbing, the auth, the tool-chaining behaviour
and the mess-case handling. What it does not prove: that the portfolio is
plausible. That waits on the generator fix.
